ØKT is designed around one idea: logging your workout should take zero mental energy.

Automatic rest timers
Log a set and the rest timer fires instantly. Countdown, color shift, haptic buzz — back to work without thinking.
📊
Previous data on the card
See "Last: 75 kg × 8 · 3 sets" directly on the exercise card. No tapping into history — progressive overload is frictionless.

Core timer mode
Configure work and rest intervals once. Timer counts down your work, rests automatically, and queues the next set.

Calendar planning
Plan workouts ahead with exercises, sets, and notes. On the day, one tap converts your plan into a live session.
