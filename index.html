ØKT is designed around one idea: logging your workout should take zero mental energy.

Automatic rest timers
Log a set and the rest timer fires instantly. Countdown, colour shift, haptic buzz — back to work without thinking.
Previous data on the card
See “Last: 75 kg × 8 · 3 sets” directly on the exercise card. No tapping into history — progressive overload is frictionless.
Core timer mode
Configure work and rest intervals once. The timer counts down your work, rests automatically, and queues the next set.
Calendar planning
Plan workouts ahead with exercises, sets, and notes. On the day, one tap converts your plan into a live session.
Progress charts
Strength volume, cardio distance, and body measurements tracked over time. Weekly goals with streak history.
Full exercise library
150+ exercises across press, pull, leg, core, and cardio. Filter by movement, muscle group, or equipment.

Privacy by architecture

No account. No server.
No tracking.

Your workout data is written to your device and nowhere else. There is no login, no analytics SDK, and nothing for anyone to sell — because it never leaves the phone in the first place.

Read the full privacy policy
  • Stored locally on your device only
  • No account or login required
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Deleting the app deletes everything
