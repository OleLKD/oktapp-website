Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

ØKT is a workout logging application designed to help users track their training sessions.

Information We Collect

ØKT stores workout information entered by the user, including exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, training history, and profile information.

Data Storage

All workout data is stored locally on the user's device.

ØKT does not require user accounts.

ØKT does not transmit workout data to our servers.

Third-Party Services

ØKT may use platform services provided by Apple, Google, Expo, or other app-distribution services.

Children's Privacy

ØKT is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.
