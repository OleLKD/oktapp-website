Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version
  • Everything you enter about your clients is stored on your device only
  • We have no server and no access to your clients' data
  • No account or login, no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Your subscription is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details
  • You are responsible for your clients' personal data — this page explains what that means
01

Introduction

ØKT PT (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a coaching app for iOS that lets personal trainers manage their clients, training programmes and sessions. This Privacy Policy explains how ØKT PT handles information. It is separate from the privacy policy for the ØKT workout logger.

The app does not require an account, does not connect to any server of ours to store your data, and does not include any advertising or analytics infrastructure.

02

Who is responsible for what

You, the coach, decide why and how your clients' information is used. That makes you responsible for it under data-protection law (for example the GDPR). ØKT PT is a tool you use for that; we provide the app but never receive, store or process your clients' data.

This means you should tell your clients what you record about them, only record what you need, and handle any request from a client to see, correct or delete their information yourself. Because we hold no copy, we cannot do this for you.

03

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter into ØKT PT is stored on your device using the operating system's local storage. Depending on how you use the app, this can include:

  • Your clients' names and any contact details you enter
  • Notes you write about clients
  • Body measurements and progress records
  • Training programmes, planned sessions and logged sessions
  • Billing information you record for your clients
  • Your own profile, settings and preferences

This data never leaves your device through ØKT PT. It is not sent to any ØKT server, not shared with third parties by us, and not accessible to us in any form.

Some of this information, such as body measurements or notes with health-related details, can be sensitive. Keep your device protected with a passcode and keep iOS up to date.

04

Data we do not collect

ØKT PT does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following to us or to anyone else:

  • Your name, email or phone number, or any information about your clients
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioural data
  • Crash reports sent to third-party services
  • Payment card or other payment details
05

Subscriptions and payments

ØKT PT may offer features through an auto-renewing subscription. Subscriptions are bought, charged and managed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). Apple handles your payment details and purchase history under its own privacy policy. We never see your card or payment details.

The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

06

Backups

If you use iCloud backup or an encrypted device backup, your device's backup may include the data stored by ØKT PT, including your clients' information. These backups are managed entirely by Apple under its own privacy policy; ØKT has no involvement in or access to them.

07

Third-party services and this website

ØKT PT does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data processing services. The only platform service involved is Apple's App Store, as described above.

This website (oktapp.com) is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps its normal server logs. It uses no cookies and no analytics, and stores only your language choice in your browser. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your IP address is sent to Google when a page is opened, under Google's privacy policy.

08

Children and minors

ØKT PT is intended for use by coaches, not by children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you coach clients under 18, you are responsible for handling their information appropriately, including obtaining any parental consent that applies where you live.

09

Data deletion

Because all data is stored on your device, you control it at all times. Deleting the app deletes the data it stores on your device, including all client records, permanently.

Deletion cannot be undone, and we cannot restore data for you, since we never had a copy. Make sure you have what you need before removing the app or changing devices. There is no account to delete and no server-side data to request removal of.

10

Changes to this policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify users through an app update. If ØKT PT's data practices change, for example if a new feature uses data differently, we will update this page before the change takes effect.

11

Contact

post@oktapp.com
