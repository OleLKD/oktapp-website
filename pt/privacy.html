Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version
  • Your clients' data is stored on your device. We have no server, no account system and no access to it.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Data leaves your device only when you choose to share it: codes and QR codes, PDFs, exports and backups. Some of these contain personal data and are not encrypted.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • You are responsible for your clients' personal data. This page explains what that means in practice.
01

Introduction

ØKT PT (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a coaching app for iOS that lets personal trainers manage their clients, training programmes, sessions, schedule and billing. This Privacy Policy explains how ØKT PT handles information. It is separate from the privacy policy for the ØKT workout logger.

The app has no user account and does not send your data to any server of ours.

02

Who is responsible for what

You, the coach, decide why and how your clients' information is used. That makes you responsible for it under data-protection law (for example the GDPR). ØKT PT is a tool you use for that. We provide the app, but we do not receive, store or process your clients' data.

Information about a client's health, such as health answers, pain notes or body measurements, is often treated as sensitive under data-protection law. You need a lawful basis, and often explicit consent, to record it. The app lets you note the date a client gave consent, but it does not collect or verify consent for you.

You should tell your clients what you record about them, record only what you need, and handle requests from clients to see, correct or delete their information yourself. We hold no copy, so we cannot do this for you.

We are responsible only for what we handle ourselves: emails you send to us, and the server logs of this website.

03

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored on your device, in the app's database and in the iOS Keychain. Depending on how you use the app, this can include:

  • Your clients' names and the contact details you enter (free text, for example an email or phone number)
  • Date of birth, emergency contact, goals and health screening answers and notes
  • Check-ins, readiness notes (energy, sleep, pain) and body measurements
  • Session logs, programmes, appointments, tasks and the notes you write about clients and sessions
  • Billing records, and issued receipts, invoices and credit notes
  • Your own business details (name, organisation number, address, bank details) and settings

ØKT PT does not send this data to us. It stays on your device unless you choose to share it (see below). We cannot read it.

Some of it can be sensitive. Protect your device with a passcode and keep iOS up to date. Depending on your iOS settings, iCloud or device backups may include the app's data. Those backups are managed by Apple under its own privacy policy.

04

Sharing, exports and backups

ØKT PT never sends your data anywhere by itself. These are the ways data can leave the app, all started by you:

  • Codes and QR codes for the ØKT athlete app. A client code contains the client's name, any email or phone number you entered, programme, schedule and start date. Session and homework codes contain training content and the client's first name. Codes are readable by anyone who has them; they are not encrypted.
  • QR codes contain a link to oktapp.com/i. If a QR code is scanned with the iPhone camera and the ØKT athlete app does not open it, the link opens in the browser and the code, being part of the web address, reaches this website's host (GitHub Pages), where it can appear in normal server logs.
  • PDF receipts, invoices, credit notes and income reports. These contain your business details (including bank details), the client's name and contact details, and amounts.
  • Client data export (a zip file). It contains everything about one client, including health-related profile information, and, if you leave the option on, your private notes. It is not encrypted.
  • Encrypted backups. Backup files are encrypted with a key stored in your device's Keychain. Without your recovery code a backup cannot be opened on another device, so keep the code safe. Only technical information such as the file format version and creation time is readable without the key.
  • Payment links. If you add a payment link, tapping it opens your payment provider's page and includes the client's first name and the amount in the link.
  • Reminders. Task reminders are shown on your device only. They can include the task text and the client's name on the lock screen.

You choose where these go (Messages, AirDrop, Mail, Files, iCloud Drive, other apps). Once shared, they are outside our control and outside ØKT PT. Send them only to the person they are meant for.

05

Camera, Face ID and notifications

Camera: used only to scan QR codes from the ØKT athlete app. The camera is asked for when you open the scanner. Only the code is read; no photos or video are stored or sent.

Face ID, Touch ID or passcode: used only to show your backup recovery code. This is handled by iOS; we never receive biometric data.

Notifications: used only for local reminders you set. There is no push notification service and no server involved.

06

Data we do not collect

ØKT PT does not send any of the following to us or to anyone else by itself (see section 04 for what you choose to share):

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any information about your clients
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking data
  • Usage analytics or behavioural data
  • Crash reports through any third-party service
  • Payment card or other payment details

The app creates a random technical device ID, stored on your device, to keep track of the order of edits. It can appear in backups and exports, but it is never sent to us. If you have chosen in iOS settings to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage statistics that do not identify you.

07

Subscriptions and payments

ØKT PT is a subscription app. An active subscription (or free trial) is needed to add and edit data. If it ends, the app becomes read-only: you can still view, export and back up your data.

Subscriptions are bought, charged and managed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details and purchase history under its own privacy policy. We never see your card or payment details. The app reads your subscription status from Apple on your device and keeps a copy of that status (plan and dates) in the Keychain. You can manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

08

Deleting data

Archiving a client keeps all their data.

'Erase personal data' for an archived client removes the client's name and contact details and deletes their sessions, goals, check-ins, measurements, homework, tasks and appointment notes. It does not remove payment records or issued receipts, invoices and credit notes, which contain the client's name and contact details, because you may be legally required to keep accounting records for a number of years. Check the rules that apply to you.

Erase does not remove backups you have saved, exports and files you have shared, codes you have sent, or copies in iCloud or device backups.

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. It does not remove those other copies, and some items in the iOS Keychain may remain. We cannot delete anything for you, because we hold no copy.

09

Children and minors

ØKT PT is intended for coaches, not for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you coach clients under 18, you are responsible for handling their information appropriately, including any parental consent that applies where you live.

10

Third parties and this website

ØKT PT contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. Apple's App Store handles subscriptions. If you add a payment link, your chosen payment provider handles that page.

This website (oktapp.com) is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps its normal server logs. It uses no cookies and no analytics and stores only your language choice in your browser. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your IP address is sent to Google when a page is opened, under Google's privacy policy.

11

Changes to this policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify users through an app update. If ØKT PT's data practices change, for example if a new feature uses data differently, we will update this page before the change takes effect.
